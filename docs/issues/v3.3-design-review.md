# v3.3 Design Review Issues

Source: user playtest/design review, 2026-05-22. Status: source-fixed, live-pending.

## Scope

- Vakuu: redesign Challenge Vakuu as a greed fight with Stolen Locks, Blood Debt, contract choice, and a cash-out option.
- Closed Court: reduce turn-1 burst. Proposed: no standard card rewards; turn 1 draw 4 and gain 2 Energy; turn 4 draw 2 and gain 2 Energy.
- Mirror line: simplify Mirror Rebuttal into a fixed mirror-card effect; keep Mirror Hall Echo as last-card-type echo with shorter text.
- After Rain: replace one-shot fatal insurance with Act 1 Rain Breath support and Act 2 payoff based on trigger count.
- Seedbed: current follow-up design uses `Temporary` and `Plant`: set Seedbed slots, then future temporary negative cards and Blight Sprouts are planted before entering hand. Upgraded Seedbed can also plant one eligible card from draw/discard immediately.
- Lotha text pass: shorten Single Sentence, Death Reprieve, Deferred Verdict, and Public Evidence without changing mechanics unless testing demands it.

## Proposed Player Text

- Vakuu: "Break Stolen Locks for Gold and extra blessings. Contracts break locks faster, but add Blood Debt. After breaking a lock, you may cash out."
- Closed Court: "No standard card rewards. Turn 1: draw 4, gain 2 Energy. Turn 4: draw 2, gain 2 Energy."
- Mirror Rebuttal: "Choose 1 mirror card. On your first turn after normal draw, move it into your hand. The first time you play it each combat, Attacks and Skills play 1 extra time; Powers cost 0."
- Mirror Hall Echo: "At turn end, remember the last card type you played. Next turn, your first matching card echoes: Attacks and Skills play 1 extra time; Powers cost 0 and draw 1."
- After Rain: "In Act 1, after the first unblocked attack damage each combat, gain 1 Rain Breath. At Act 2 start, if this triggered fewer than 3 times, gain 75 Gold; otherwise heal 8 and upgrade 1 card."
- Seedbed: "Gain Block. Set up 2 Seedbed slots. Later, Temporary Status cards, Temporary Curse cards, or Blight Sprouts that would enter your hand are planted first. Add 1 Withered Husk for each card planted. Exhaust."
- Seedbed+: "Gain Block. Set up 3 Seedbed slots. Immediately plant 1 matching card from your draw or discard pile. Later matching cards are planted first."
- Single Sentence: "Each turn, your first Attack or Skill plays 2 extra times. Then you may play up to 4 more cards this turn. Powers do not count, cost 0, and draw 1."
- Death Reprieve: "Once per run, prevent death and set HP to 1. Take one final turn: draw 10, gain 10 Energy, cards cost 0, and you cannot die. At turn end, if enemies remain, die."
- Deferred Verdict: "At turn 4 start, draw 4, gain 4 Energy and 3 Verdict. This turn, each non-Status card spends 1 Verdict: Attacks and Skills play 1 extra time; Powers cost 0 and draw 1. If combat ends before turn 4, heal 4."
- Public Evidence: "Your non-damage debuffs apply twice and grant 1 Enlightenment. Enemy non-damage debuffs on you also apply twice and remove 1 Enlightenment. At turn start, spend up to 3 Enlightenment; each draws 1 and gives 4 Block."

## Design Notes

- Text should follow source-game style: short trigger, effect, limit.
- Avoid implementation wording such as "visible count", "if possible", "fallback", and long source explanations.
- Vakuu rewards should be Loot Gold plus extra Ancient blessing choices, not normal card rewards.
- Seedbed should not trigger play, discard, draw, or Exhaust synergies for planted cards.
- Rootblight and Withered Husk are not plantable. Blight Sprout is plantable and will not grow when planted.
- Public Evidence is text-only for now; if players avoid it, consider limiting enemy debuff doubling to once per turn.

## Implementation Notes, 2026-05-22

- Vakuu now offers contract choices on turns 1/3/5, adds Cash Out after a lock breaks, settles Blood Debt against loot, and ends through the existing no-normal-reward victory path.
- Closed Court now gives turn 1 draw 4 / Energy 2 and turn 4 draw 2 / Energy 2; the old first-three-card discount was removed.
- Mirror Rebuttal now uses one fixed mirror card; Attack/Skill play 1 extra time and Power cards cost 0 only.
- After Rain now adds Rain Breath after the first unblocked enemy attack damage in Act 1 combats, then pays Gold or heal+upgrade at Act 2 start.
- Seedbed now stores 2/3 combat slots. `TryPlantSeedbedCardFromHand` plants eligible future cards before they resolve in hand; Seedbed+ can immediately plant one eligible draw/discard card with `CardPileCmd.RemoveFromCombat`.
- Final source review fixed the follow-up risks: Vakuu damage-lock tracking now uses `AfterDamageGiven` so lethal hits are counted, Cash Out has a hand-full immediate-choice fallback, Seedbed uses Temporary/Plant hover text and skips Rootblight plus Withered Husk, Mirror Rebuttal says the card is moved rather than drawn, and visible Vakuu text now says Contract choices appear on turns 1/3/5.
